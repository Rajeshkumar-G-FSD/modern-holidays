FAQ

Visa Questions, Answered

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa decisions are made solely by the respective embassy, consulate or immigration authority. We help you prepare a complete, accurate application to give it the best chance.

How early should I apply?

It depends on the country and season. As a general rule, start at least 4–8 weeks before travel for sticker visas (such as Schengen, UK or USA) and allow time for appointments. E-visas are often quicker — we’ll advise based on your destination.

Can you help if I am not booking a holiday with you?

Yes. Visa assistance is available as a standalone service. Many travellers also choose to combine it with flights, hotels and insurance.

What are your service charges?

Service charges depend on the country and visa type. Embassy or government fees are separate and paid as per official rates. Contact us for a clear quotation.

Do I need to visit your office?

Not always. Many steps can be handled over phone, WhatsApp and email. Some countries require in-person biometrics at a visa application centre — we’ll guide you through it.
